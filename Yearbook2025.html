Father of IT Education in the Philippines

ACLC College of Taytay, established in 1997, began its journey at the Manila East Arcade Building in Brgy. San Juan, Taytay, Rizal. As the institution grew, it found a permanent home at #7 El Monteverde Subdivision in the same barangay. From its humble beginnings, the college has blossomed into a beacon of high-quality education, steadfast in its mission to empower students with strong academic skills while nurturing a deep and abiding faith in God.


In 2012, the implementation of the K-12 system in the Philippines presented both a challenge and a new horizon of opportunities. Rising to the occasion, ACLC College of Taytay expanded its programs to include Senior High School by Academic Year 2016-2017, offering strands in Accountancy, Business and Management (ABM); Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM); and Technical-Vocational Information and Communication Technology (ICT). The college set a target enrollment of 800 students but surpassed expectations with 1,299 enrollees—a remarkable achievement that earned the AMA Education System the prestigious "Shining Star Award" for exceeding its goals.


As a proud member of the AMA Education System, a trailblazer in IT education, ACLC College of Taytay embraced the "Online Blended Learning" approach introduced in 2015. This innovative method revolutionized the learning experience, opening new doors for student engagement and academic excellence. The college's proactive adoption of this technology not only enhanced its educational delivery but also contributed to its growing enrollment and reputation.


When the COVID-19 pandemic struck in 2019, ACLC College of Taytay stood resilient. Leveraging the online platform it had already established, the college swiftly transitioned to fully online learning, ensuring that education remained uninterrupted. Its pioneering spirit in online education positioned the institution as a model of adaptability and forward-thinking in the Philippine educational landscape.


Today, ACLC College of Taytay remains unwavering in its commitment to excellence and service. With every milestone, the institution continues to grow and evolve, guided by its core values and an unshakeable faith in the Almighty God—the Lord Jesus Christ—our guide, strength, provider, and sustainer. As we look to the future, we are inspired to dream bigger, reach higher, and shine brighter.
